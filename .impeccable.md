## Design Context

### Users
A traveler or local with a dietary restriction choosing where to eat, plus hackathon judges watching a live demo. The job is “can I eat here, and why should I trust that?” The Jev trace is for the second half: show the typed decisions that produced the answer, including uncertainty.

### Brand Personality
Calibrated, forensic, honest. Never salesy. Never round a maybe up to a yes.

### Aesthetic Direction
The product UI stays a light working tool. The Jev trace is a separate inspection window: warm dark, kitchen-ticket rhythm, stamped confidence, receipt-like quotes. Turmeric and sage accents, not cyan or purple. Anti-reference: generic admin “activity log,” glowing AI dashboards, gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. Show only decisions Jev actually made. Missing values say “not reported.”
2. Confidence and alternatives are first-class, not footnotes.
3. Evidence quotes are context, never proof an allergen is absent.
4. Motion explains sequence (steps arriving in order), then stays out of the way.
5. Phone-readable: stack tickets, keep the stamp and the choice readable without hovering.
